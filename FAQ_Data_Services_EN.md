## 💬 Detailed Answers

### 🤝 Approach and Collaboration

#### 1. What is the difference between a Data Analyst and a Data Engineer?
The **Data Engineer** builds the infrastructure: they clean, organize, and merge data from different software systems into a single, secure database. The **Data Analyst** takes this clean data and transforms it into charts, reports, and interactive dashboards to help the business owner make decisions. Having expertise in both fields allows me to manage the entire process from start to finish.

#### 2. Is my company too small for these types of services?
No. If you use at least one management software, an e-commerce platform, or track sales and inventory on Excel, you already have enough data to optimize your business. The **START** package is specifically designed for micro-businesses that want to get started without a large budget.

#### 3. Do you work remotely or on-site?
I perform most of the work **remotely**, which minimizes downtime and optimizes project costs. We will schedule regular alignment and screen-sharing calls. For specific needs of structured medium-sized enterprises, on-site operational sessions can be evaluated.

#### 4. What is the typical workflow of a project?
The standard path consists of four phases:
* **Audit:** Analysis of your current systems and definition of KPIs.
* **Engineering:** Data connection and cleaning.
* **Analysis:** Creation of visual dashboards.
* **Training:** Delivery and handoff to your team to ensure autonomy.

#### 5. Do you offer ongoing support after the project is completed?
Yes. After delivery, I provide a warranty period to ensure everything runs smoothly. Afterward, you can choose the **Fractional Data Manager** service to have fixed strategic and technical support each month, without the overhead costs of a full-time hire.

### 🛠️ Technical Aspects and Tools

#### 6. Which tools and technologies do you mainly use?
I use modern, flexible, and industry-standard tools. For data visualization, I rely on **Microsoft Power BI** and **Google Looker Studio**. For data engineering, integration, and manipulation, I use **SQL** and **Python**, supported by secure cloud infrastructures.

#### 7. Will I need to purchase expensive software licenses?
No. For most SMEs, I aim to leverage free tools or software already included in your corporate suites (such as the standard version of Power BI or Looker Studio), eliminating or minimizing recurring third-party fixed costs.

#### 8. What does "Data Mapping" actually mean?
It is the creation of a logical "bridge" between different software systems. For instance, if your e-commerce platform labels a customer as "Customer_Email" and your point-of-sale (POS) system calls them "Cod_Contatto", data mapping unifies these fields under a single label, preventing duplicates and faulty reports.

#### 9. What is a Data Pipeline (ETL) and what is it used for?
ETL stands for Extract, Transform, and Load. It is an automated workflow that extracts raw data from your software, removes errors and duplicates, and moves it into the final database ready for data visualization, eliminating all manual work.

#### 10. Can we integrate data from disorganized Excel spreadsheets?
Yes. One of a Data Engineer's primary tasks is to take fragmented Excel files, clean them using automated scripts, and integrate them with official company software data.

### 📊 Business Value and Timelines

#### 11. What are the concrete benefits for my SME?
The main benefits are the **elimination of human errors** caused by manual copy-pasting, **time savings** for your staff, and the ability to instantly see which products or customers are driving your real profit margins.

#### 12. How long does it take to see the first reports ready?
For a **START** package, the first reports are ready in about **2-3 weeks**. For more complex projects like the **GROWTH** package (which involves the automated integration of multiple sources), timelines vary between **4 and 8 weeks**.

#### 13. How do you define KPIs (Key Performance Indicators) for my industry?
We define KPIs together during the initial audit phase. We will isolate only the metrics that truly impact your revenue and costs (e.g., Customer Lifetime Value, inventory turnover, net margin), avoiding complex but useless charts.

#### 14. Can I monitor data in real-time, or are there delays?
It depends on your business needs and the software you use. Usually, a scheduled refresh (e.g., once or twice a day) is ideal and highly sustainable for an SME. If the business model demands it, near-real-time streams (**real-time feeds**) can be configured.

#### 15. Are the dashboards easy for my team to use?
Absolutely. Dashboards are designed to be highly **intuitive and interactive**. Anyone on your team can click a simple filter (such as a month of the year or a product category) and see the charts update instantly, with no technical skills required.

### 🔐 Security, Privacy, and Budget

#### 16. How is the security and privacy of business data guaranteed?
Data is processed following the strictest cybersecurity standards and in full compliance with **GDPR**. Database access is protected by encrypted credentials and restricted only to authorized personnel.

#### 17. Do we sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) before starting?
Yes. Before receiving any credentials, files containing business data, or vendor/customer information, we will sign a **Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)** to fully protect your intellectual property and your business.

#### 18. How do payments work for the Start and Growth packages?
One-off projects typically follow a milestone-based payment structure: an initial deposit to start the project (e.g., 40%), an intermediate progress payment, and a final balance upon delivery and approval of the dashboards.

#### 19. What happens if my data is stored in an old or legacy ERP system?
In most cases, it is still possible to extract data through automated exports (CSV/Excel) or by directly querying the software's underlying database. We will analyze the technical feasibility during the initial audit.

#### 20. What is a Fractional Data Manager service and when is it beneficial?
It is a fixed-fee monthly retainer service that embeds a data expert into your team for a set number of hours per month. It is highly beneficial for growing companies that need to constantly update reports, analyze new metrics, and receive ongoing strategic support.
